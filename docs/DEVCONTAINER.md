# samsara-roguelike

A 2D roguelike about a soul moving through the labyrinth of rebirth.
Three stats — Power, Speed, Luck — whose values are conserved across a run
but reshuffled every time you step through a rift. Items persist; your
configuration doesn't. Half of them lie dormant until the right life.
